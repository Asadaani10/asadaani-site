Affiliate Disclosure

Plain-language explanation of how this site earns money, and what it means for the reviews you read here.

Short version

Asadaani participates in affiliate programs, including with web hosting companies such as Bluehost, Hostgator, and Hostinger. When you click certain links on this site and make a purchase, we may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you.

What that means for our content

Commission potential does not determine our rankings. Hosts are evaluated on pricing transparency, measured speed and uptime, dashboard usability, and support responsiveness. Where a host performs worse in testing, that's stated directly, even if it's a program we're part of.

How to identify affiliate content

Any page containing affiliate links includes a visible disclosure notice near the top of the content, and affiliate calls-to-action are marked accordingly. This page serves as the general, site-wide version of that same notice.

Editorial independence

No hosting company reviewed on this site has paid for a review, previewed a review before publication, or been guaranteed a specific ranking or score.
