The short version

Hostinger remains our top pick for budget shared hosting after re-testing plans this year. The intro pricing is aggressive, but unlike a lot of competitors, the renewal price doesn't jump to an unreasonable multiple — and performance held up consistently across our test period.

Pricing and plans

Hostinger's shared hosting comes in three tiers — Single, Premium, and Business — scaling up in website count, storage, and included features like daily backups. The Premium tier is the sweet spot for most small sites: one website's worth of resources at the Single tier is tight if you're running WordPress with several plugins.

Speed and uptime

Across our test period, average Time to First Byte landed around 210ms from a US test location, with uptime holding at 99.98%. That's a meaningfully better result than the other two hosts in our comparison.

Dashboard and ease of use

Hostinger uses its own custom control panel (hPanel) rather than the more dated cPanel interface most competitors use. It's noticeably cleaner for beginners — the 1-click WordPress installer, file manager, and domain tools are all easy to find without digging through menus.

Support

Live chat is available 24/7. In our pre-sales test, average first response time was under two minutes, which was faster than both other hosts we compared.

Where it falls short

  • No phone support — chat and ticket only
  • Weekly backups on the entry Single plan (daily backups require Premium or higher)
  • Data center selection is limited on the lowest tier

Who it's best for

Anyone starting a first website, a blog, or a small business site who wants strong performance without overpaying. If you need a US-based phone support line or are running a large multi-site agency setup, it's worth comparing against higher-tier managed hosting instead.
