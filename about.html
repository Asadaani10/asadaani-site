About Asadaani

Reviews written by someone who actually manages websites.

Asadaani started as a set of personal notes on hosting, deployment, and small-site tooling — and turned into a resource for anyone trying to cut through marketing copy.

Why this site exists

Most "best web hosting" content online is written to sell, not to inform — long lists ranked by affiliate commission rather than real testing. Asadaani takes a narrower approach: fewer hosts, tested more honestly, with the trade-offs stated plainly.

Articles here also cover the practical side of running a small site — deployment, performance, and the tools that actually matter once the hosting decision is made.

How reviews are put together

  • Sign up for the plan being reviewed, same as any reader would
  • Measure real load times and uptime over a testing period
  • Time actual support response, not published SLAs
  • Read the renewal pricing and cancellation terms directly from the host's own policy pages
